ABOUT US

GRANDMA'S BAKERIA

We are a small family bakery. Everything comes from Grandma's handwritten recipe cards, baked in small batches with simple ingredients.

Every fall, her Fall Parfait comes back to the counter. Its recipe changes every week, and this game is how you guess it.

HOW IT WORKS

  • Fill the cup with the 5 ingredients you think Grandma used.
  • Taste it. Green layers are in the recipe, red ones are not.
  • Crack it in 2 tries or fewer: free parfait.
  • 3 or 4 tries: free coffee with any parfait.
  • 5 or more tries: 15% off your parfait.
  • Each win earns a loyalty stamp. 10 stamps is a free parfait.
